Posture openness signal

Testing whether a simple geometric heuristic on MediaPipe body-pose landmarks — shoulder width, arm openness, head/shoulder drop — can produce a usable "openness" score, matching the pattern this review paper described: expansive posture reads positive, diminutive/contracted posture reads negative. No training involved; every number below is computed directly from your live pose keypoints.
