Geometric landmark signal

This paper doesn't release a trained model — it describes a methodology: track how far facial landmarks (mouth corners, eyebrows, eyes) move from a calibrated neutral position, using known associations between specific movements and emotions. This is a real implementation of that described approach, using 68-point facial landmarks, to see whether it behaves differently from your CNN under the same lighting/webcam conditions that gave the CNN trouble.

"The wide opening of the mouth can infer happiness, anger, and disgust. In contrast, fear and sadness can be recognized through the eyes and surprise can be inferred by widening eyes, mouth, and forehead." — Plutchik (1962), as cited in the reference paper. "Geometric-based methods are invariant to face, age, pose, and illumination and give better accuracy than appearance-based methods." — same paper.
