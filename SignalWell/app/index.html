What this tool is — and isn't

SignalWell is a screening aid built for a student project. It estimates a mood signal from expression, voice, text and habits. It does not diagnose anxiety, depression, or any clinical condition, and a low reading is a prompt to reach out to a person, not a verdict.

Any real deployment on a campus should sit behind informed consent, a named counselor in the loop, and ethics review — many users will be minors.

Open-source model stack
